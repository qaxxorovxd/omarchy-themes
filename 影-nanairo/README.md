# 影 Nanairo

Neutral ink · the wallpaper picks the colour

Installed as `影-nanairo`, shown in the theme menu as **影 Nanairo**.
Apply it with `omarchy-theme-set 影-nanairo`.

The other nine themes in this pack work the other way round: a hue is chosen and
the wallpapers are matched to it. Here the palette gets out of the way. The
accent is a silver, the greys carry almost no hue, and the ANSI colours are left
untinted, so nothing on screen argues with the picture behind it — and the
63 wallpapers are deliberately *not* colour-graded. Each one keeps the
palette it was drawn with, so cycling backgrounds recolours the desk, not the UI.

Every wallpaper was picked for a clean, focused composition — one subject, little
clutter, a calm background — and no two share a colour vibe. Subjects: 20 anime, 12 nature, 9 minimal, 8 tech, 7 place, 5 arch, 2 other.
